Help & Official Rules

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: When do weekly picks lock?

Picks lock at a single time the Commissioner sets for the whole week — not separately for each game. You can change your picks and your tiebreaker prediction as many times as you like right up until that lock time hits.

Q: How does weekly scoring work?

It's straight-up winner picks — you get credit for every game where your pick matches the final winner. There are no confidence points, spread picks, or multipliers; every correct pick counts the same.

Q: What happens if a game ends in a tie, or is canceled or postponed?

The game is closed out as having no winner. Nobody gets credit for their pick on that game either way — it's excluded entirely from both the weekly and season standings, for everyone. If a final score exists (as with a tie), it's still shown on the game card even though the game doesn't count toward anyone's record.

Q: What happens if I don't finish all my picks before the deadline?

Be careful here — Save Picks only saves anything once every game for the week has a pick. Tapping a team just stages your pick on screen; nothing is actually recorded until you click Save Picks with the whole week filled in. If the lock time passes before you've picked every game and clicked Save, you get no credit for the week at all — even for the games you did pick. There's also no auto-pick feature, so make sure every game has a selection, then click Save Picks well before the deadline.

Q: How is the weekly tiebreaker calculated?

Each week, predict the combined total points scored by both teams in the Monday Night game. If two or more players end the week with the same number of correct picks, whoever's prediction is closest to the actual combined score ranks higher for that week.

Q: How do leaderboard rankings and ties work?

Rankings use Standard Competition Ranking — the same method used across most professional sports — so ties are handled fairly on both leaderboards.

Season standings are ranked purely by total correct picks. If two players are tied on correct picks, they share the same rank.

Weekly standings work the same way, except the Monday Night tiebreaker prediction breaks a tie first — two players only share a weekly rank if they're tied on both correct picks and tiebreaker closeness.

Why ranks skip: when players share a rank, the next rank down accounts for all of them. For example:

  • Player A: 14 correct picks → 1st Place
  • Player B: 14 correct picks → 1st Place (tied)
  • Player C: 13 correct picks → 3rd Place (2nd is skipped — two players already hold 1st)
  • Player D: 12 correct picks → 4th Place

This way your rank always reflects exactly how many people are genuinely ahead of you.

Q: I forgot my password — how do I get back in?

On the sign-in screen, tap "Forgot password?" and enter the email address tied to your account. You'll get an automated password reset email — click the link in it to set a new password, then sign in as usual.

That email comes from Supabase, the automated system that runs logins behind the scenes for the site — not a custom Pickems Club address, so it may look a little plain or generic in your inbox. That's expected; it's legitimate. Give it a few minutes to arrive, and check your spam/junk folder if you don't see it.

If it's been a while and you still don't have the email, reach out to the Commissioner at thepickemsclub@gmail.com — they can have the system Admin reset your password for you manually.

Official League Rules

  1. 1

    One Account Per Person — each player may operate a single account. Creating multiple accounts to test different pick combinations is not allowed and may result in disqualification.

  2. 2

    Strict Lock-in Deadlines — all picks must be submitted through the site before the week's lock time. Late submissions by text, chat, or any other channel won't be accepted under any circumstances.

  3. 3

    Fair Play & Sportsmanship — respect fellow league members in chat and everywhere else. Harassment or malicious behavior can result in a chat ban or removal from the club.

  4. 4

    Results Are Automatic, Never Manual — every game result is pulled in automatically from ESPN's scoreboard data; nobody, including the Commissioner, can manually type in a score or declare a winner. Once a game is graded, that result is final. A tied, canceled, or postponed game is closed out by the Commissioner as having no winner — never as a win for either team. (This uses an unofficial ESPN API, so on rare occasions auto-updating can pause temporarily — see How It Works for details.)

  5. 5

    New Players Are Always Welcome — most of the club already knows each other, and we want it to keep growing. If you invite someone the rest of the group doesn't know, you're vouching for them — you're on the hook for making sure they understand the rules, pay any dues on time, and play fair. So invite away, just keep an eye on your plus-one.
