How The Pickems Club Works

Everything the site does, in plain language — how picks are made, how scoring works, and what the Commissioner tools do behind the scenes.

Making Picks

  • Each week, tap a team card to pick who you think will win — team logos included, so it's a one-tap pick per game. There is a drop down to chose the current week, please make sure you are onthe currect week.
  • Picks lock at a single time the Commissioner sets for the whole week (not separately per game at each kickoff). Lock time can be seen in the "Week" section.
  • Once a pick is locked, it's shown but can no longer be changed — no editing after the lock time.
  • You can change your mind and re-pick as many times as you like before the lock time hits.
  • The system will not let you save your picks until you have completed all the picks. The system will let you save the tie breaker score if you do not enter one. There are two separate saves; one for the picks and one for the tie breaker.
✅

How Scoring Works

Once a game ends, your pick is graded automatically — no action needed from you. Here's what you'll see on a game card:

✓ Correct

Your pick matched the final winner.

✗ Wrong

The other team won.

Final • 24–17

The game's closing score, shown on every finished game.

  • Results are pulled automatically from ESPN's live scoreboard data — never typed in by hand by anyone in the league (more on that under "Fairness" below).
  • Your season record, weekly record, and win percentage update automatically as games finish. If your score summary cards on the picks page do not refresh, use the "refresh" button to update those cards.
🏆

Monday Night Tiebreaker

  • Every week, predict the combined total points scored by both teams in the Monday night game(s).
  • This only comes into play when two or more players are tied on correct picks for the week — whoever's prediction is closest to the actual combined score wins that week.
  • The tiebreaker prediction locks at the same time as your regular picks for the week.
📊

Standings & Personal Stats

  • Season standings — total correct picks and win percentage across every week, always visible.
  • Weekly standings — correct picks, win percentage, and tiebreaker result for any single week you select.
  • Your own record and rank (this week and season-long) are shown right on the picks page, so you don't have to dig for them.
🔄

Live Updates

  • The site checks in the background for new scores and lock-time changes, so standings and pick statuses stay current without you doing anything.
  • There's also a manual Refresh button if you want the very latest right away — handy right after a game just ended.
Heads up: automatic score updates run through an unofficial ESPN scoreboard API — not something ESPN officially publishes for outside use. On rare occasions ESPN may block automated requests from our server, which would pause auto-updating temporarily or permanently. If that happens, the Commissioner can pull the same data in manually with the Sync Scores button (see "Commissioner Tools" below), which works reliably since it runs as a normal request from their browser instead of our server.
💬

League Chat

  • A live group chat for the whole league — talk trash, celebrate, commiserate.
  • An unread badge appears on the Chat tab when there are new messages you haven't seen yet. There are no phone or browser push notifications — it's an in-app indicator only.
🤝

Ties, Cancelled & Postponed Games

NFL ties are rare, but they happen — and every so often a game gets cancelled or postponed indefinitely. Here's how those are handled:

  • If a game ends in a tie, the final score is still recorded automatically from ESPN's scoreboard data — you'll see it on the game card.
  • The Commissioner then closes the game out as a tie, or as voided if it was cancelled/postponed. Either way, it's marked as having no winner.
  • Nobody wins or loses credit for their pick on that game — it's excluded entirely from both weekly and season records, for everyone.

Commissioner Tools

A few things the Commissioner handles behind the scenes each week:

  • Import NFL Schedule — pulls that week's matchups and kickoff times automatically from the NFL's public schedule, instead of typing them in by hand.
  • Add Game Manually — for the rare case a game needs to be entered by hand instead of imported.
  • Sync Scores — checks for any finished games that haven't been graded yet and pulls in the final score from ESPN's scoreboard data. This button runs as a direct request from the Commissioner's own browser (rather than from our server), so it keeps working even in the rare case that background auto-updates get blocked — see "Live Updates" above.
  • Mark Tie / Void — closes out a game Sync Scores couldn't resolve on its own (a tie, or a cancelled/postponed game) as having no winner.
  • Lock Time Management — sets or adjusts when a week's picks and tiebreaker lock.
  • Delete Week — removes a week and its games entirely, if one was set up in error.
🔒

Fairness, By Design

  • There is no way for anyone — including the Commissioner — to manually type in a score or declare a winner. Every real result is pulled automatically from ESPN's scoreboard data.
  • That data comes from an unofficial API, so if ESPN ever blocks the automated requests, background auto-updating could pause. Even then, the fix is the Commissioner clicking Sync Scores to pull in that same automated data from their own browser — never a manual override.
  • The only manual action available at all is closing out a stuck tie or cancelled game as having no winner — it can never be used to hand a win to either team.
  • That's a deliberate choice, so results in this league are always trustworthy, whoever's running it.
